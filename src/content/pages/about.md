Fosterlite Solutions Ltd. is a Canadian software and consulting company based in Ottawa. We design, build and support custom software, web platforms and the systems that hold a business together, for clients in Canada and abroad.

We started Fosterlite because a lot of growing businesses were stuck between two bad options: generic tools that almost fit, or agencies that sell a big project, hand it over and move on. We think there's a better way to do it.
