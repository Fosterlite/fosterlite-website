Fosterlite Solutions Limited ("Fosterlite", "we") respects your privacy and handles personal information in line with Canada's _Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act_ (PIPEDA).

## What we collect

**Contact form.** When you use the contact form we collect your name, email address, company (if you give it) and your message. We use this only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about.

**Analytics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which pages are visited and how fast they load. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not collect personal information that identifies you.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## How it is processed

- The website is hosted by Netlify.
- Contact form messages are delivered to our inbox by Resend, an email delivery service.
- Spam protection on the form is provided by Cloudflare Turnstile.

These providers may process data outside Canada, including in the United States. They act on our behalf and only to provide their service.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the duration of that relationship and any legal record-keeping period. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at [contact@fosterlite.com](mailto:contact@fosterlite.com).

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
